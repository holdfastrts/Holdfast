# Fifty classical pieces: the reference table

The owner, 2026-11-14: compare the game's music "with real pieces and make an analysis of the current game music".
He sent the book *50 Most Famous Pieces of Classical Music, the Library of Piano Classics* (piano solo arrangements,
548 pages). The book is a copyrighted edition. It stays out of this public repository. Nothing from it is copied here,
only facts about each piece. Its 46 pieces are below, as read from each piece's first page in that edition.
The first page gives the key, metre, tempo mark and how the opening is written.

The pieces are old enough to be public domain, except Barber's Adagio for Strings (1936).
Our own music must never quote any of them; the study uses them only as measures to compare against.

## Groups that matter for our three sides

- **Court and Church (the Crown):** Bach's Air, Badinerie and preludes; the Mozart pieces; the Radetzky March; the
  Strauss waltzes; Chopin's polonaise; Liszt's Bénédiction; the Dies Irae and Vissi d'arte (sacred, dramatic).
- **Machine and drive (the Clockwork):** Bach's C minor prelude, Chopin's Ocean etude and the first prelude, the
  Waldstein's pulsing chords, the Moonlight's last movement, Alkan's overture, the Figaro overture's runs.
- **Dark, primal, dance (the Brood):** Beethoven 7's processional ostinato, the Liszt sonata's opening, the Hungarian
  Rhapsody's lassan, the Trepak, Lyapunov's tarantelle, the Moonlight's first movement, the Sugar Plum Fairy's eeriness.
- **Calm (every side's peace pieces):** Clair de Lune, Gymnopédie, Bach's Air, the Swan, the Pavane, Schubert's
  Impromptu, the Girl with the Flaxen Hair.
