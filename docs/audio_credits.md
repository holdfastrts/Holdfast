# Audio credits (outside sources)

Every outside recording the game's sounds and music are made from, with the credit its licence asks for. The game has
no credits screen yet: when it gets one, these lines go in it (CC BY needs the credit; CC0 does not, but it is kind).

| Used for | Source | Author | Licence | Credit line |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The Marching Band's bagpipe | FreePats Bagpipe (art/audio_src/band/freepats_bagpipe/) | Gilles Sadowski | CC0 1.0 | Bagpipe samples by Gilles Sadowski (FreePats), CC0 |
| The Marching Band's drums | MuldjordKit, FreePats version (art/audio_src/band/muldjord_drums/) | Lars Muldjord | CC BY 4.0 | MuldjordKit drum samples by Lars Muldjord, CC BY 4.0 |
| The Marching Band's fife (the music since 0.36.6) | VCSL Baroque Soprano Recorder, Sustain (art/audio_src/band/vcsl/) | Versilian Studios (Sam Gossner) and contributors | CC0 1.0 | Recorder samples from the Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL), CC0 |
| The Marching Band's tune | Scotland the Brave, the Highland pipe setting with its gracenotes (the notes only, as text: ABC from github.com/czarxian/Silly-Wizard, marked "Traditional") | traditional | public domain (the melody) | none needed: "Scotland the Brave (traditional)" |

The band's music (assets/audio/band/*.ogg, tools/audio/make_band_music.py, docs/band_music.md) is made from the bagpipe, drum and
recorder rows above. Other jobs: add a row whenever an outside recording goes into the game (see each art/audio_src/*/SOURCES.txt).
