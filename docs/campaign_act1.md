# Act 1: The Age of Revolutions (outline)

PROPOSAL by Claude, 2026-11-11, for the owner to change freely. Plot, places, play and choices only: the dialogue is
the owner's. Canon and people: docs/campaign_timeline.md. Structure: docs/campaign_structure.md.

The Crown is at its height: the global war map is almost all purple. Small fires of rebellion flare, and two new things
appear that nobody yet fears. On the map the player picks among the open pins (option C); the braids (Crown, Clockwork,
Brood) open as the act goes on, and the act ends at the first crossing.

## Frame: the King's study, after the Prologue

The King, the heir and (from Act 1's end) old Tomasz. The heir asks why Fairleigh was so hard; the King answers with
the next story. HEIR PRACTICE (optional, short): after the Prologue, a small present-day skirmish in which the heir
holds a line until dawn, the Prologue's lesson put to use.

mission 1 carries it and a village on the map leans to the Assembly.
