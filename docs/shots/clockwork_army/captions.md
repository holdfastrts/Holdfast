# clockwork-army pictures

Filmed with tools/film_clockwork_army.gd (Frostfang, seed 11, Hard against Hard; the "before" game with `cwarmy:off`).
A film's game is not a test game: the same map and seed play a little differently each run. Pictures 03 to 06 and 11 are
from the final code; 08 to 10 (the Pendulum plan) were filmed earlier in the job, before the last three changes (the bar
after a failed push, the muster of reinforcements, the opening's soldier step), which do not change what they show.

1. `01_owner_0638_army_split.jpg`: the owner's picture (0.31.0, 6:38): the Pendulum and the Springers by the second
   base's Works, a few soldiers elsewhere, the Winders fighting riders alone.
2. `02_before_0640_army_spread_over_the_main.jpg`: the old code at 6:40, framed on every Clockwork fighter: soldiers and
   Springers spread over the main and its ramp, a few by the second base.
3. `03_after_0640_one_army_at_its_gathering_point.jpg`: the one army at 6:40, the same game time: every fighter together
   in front of the Clocktower nearest the enemy, the Raptors of a raid lying in front of it.
4. `04_after_0821_springers_jump_in_as_the_front_engages.jpg`: 8:21, the push (15 Clock Soldiers, 10 Springers) meets the
   Raptors by the second base; Springers have jumped in to shooting reach behind the front.
5. `05_after_0900_the_push_out_toward_the_brood.jpg`: 9:00, the push out on the way to the Brood's Rootmaws, framed with
   the whole base.
6. `06_v_crown_0530_together_before_it_goes.jpg`: against the Crown (Economy against Economy), 5:30: the army together at
   its gathering point, just before its first push.
7. `07_before_0817_soldiers_and_springers_strung_out.jpg`: the old code at 8:17: Clock Soldiers and Springers strung out
   in a line toward the Brood's swarm.
8. `08_pendulums_0606_the_push_at_a_rootmaw.jpg`: the Pendulum plan's push at 6:06: Pendulums, Clock Soldiers and a
   Netter together at a Rootmaw.
9. `09_pendulums_0719_netter_and_pendulums_in_the_nest.jpg`: 7:19, the push in the Brood's swamp: the Netter's net on the
   ground, the Pendulums with the Clock Soldiers.
10. `10_pendulum_push.gif`: the Pendulum plan's push from 5:45 to 8:20, every 6 s.
11. `11_v_crown_0935_the_push_at_the_crown_natural_gate.jpg`: against the Crown, 9:35: the push breaks into the Crown's
    natural through its gate, the Crown's fallen Musketeers in front of it; the rest of the block waits behind.
