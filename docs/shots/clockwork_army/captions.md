# clockwork-army pictures

Filmed with tools/film_clockwork_army.gd (Frostfang, seed 11, Hard against Hard; the "before" game with `cwarmy:off`).
The film's games are not the test games: the same map and seed play a little differently each run.

1. `01_owner_0638_army_split.jpg`: the owner's picture (0.31.0, 6:38): the Pendulum and the Springers by the second
   base's Works, a few soldiers elsewhere, the Winders fighting riders alone.
2. `02_before_0640_army_spread_over_the_main.jpg`: the old code at 6:40, framed on every Clockwork fighter: soldiers and
   Springers spread over the main and its ramp, a few by the second base.
3. `03_after_0640_one_army_at_its_gathering_point.jpg`: the one army at 6:40, the same game time: every fighter together
   in front of the Clocktower nearest the enemy.
4. `04_after_0900_whole_army_meets_the_raptors.jpg`: 9:00, a Raptor raid on the second base met by the whole army; the
   Raptors' bodies lie in front of it.
5. `05_after_0838_the_raid_met_together.jpg`: 8:38, the army fights a raid as one block.
6. `06_after_0955_the_push_goes_out_over_the_raptors.jpg`: 9:55, the push of 35 that went out at 9:40, worth more than
   the Brood army it reckoned, over the Raptors it killed on the way.
7. `07_before_0817_soldiers_and_springers_strung_out.jpg`: the old code at 8:17: Clock Soldiers and Springers strung out
   in a line toward the Brood's swarm.
8. `08_pendulums_0606_the_push_at_a_rootmaw.jpg`: the Pendulum plan's push at 6:06: Pendulums, Clock Soldiers and a
   Netter together at a Rootmaw.
9. `09_pendulums_0719_netter_and_pendulums_in_the_nest.jpg`: 7:19, the push in the Brood's swamp: the Netter's net on the
   ground, the Pendulums with the Clock Soldiers.
10. `10_pendulum_push.gif`: the Pendulum plan's push from 5:45 to 8:20, every 6 s.
11. `11_v_crown_0838_the_push_through_the_crown_gate.jpg`: against the Crown (Economy against Economy), the push breaks the
    gate of the Crown's natural and goes through as one block.
12. `12_v_crown_0530_together_before_it_goes.jpg`: 5:30 against the Crown: the army together at its gathering point, just
    before it goes out.
