# The Brood computer's base layout: pictures (docs/brood_layout.md)

Taken by `tools/study_brood_layout.gd shots:1` (xvfb, 1280 x 720, cut to 1200 px wide). Hard Brood computer (red) against
a Hard computer. "Before" = the computer before the fix (`off:1`), "after" = this branch. The owner's own picture of the
problem: `docs/playtests/2026-11-04/eco_v_eco_4_brood_turtle_traps_units.jpg`.

1. `01_redrock_before_4m00_open.jpg`: Redrock Canyon, Spitter timing against the Clockwork (seed 5), before, 4:00. The
   Brood's main is a plateau with one ramp at the top. The Mother, a Mound Turtle just above her left, Rootmaws left and
   right, the Tyrant Sire below. The way to the ramp is still open.
2. `02_redrock_before_6m01_shut_in.jpg`: the same game, 6:01: its whole base, 406 cells round the Mother, is shut in:
   the turtle above her and her nest close the ramp's mouth (the alarm's turtle rooted near the ramp at 5:55). Every
   grub, Raptor and Spitter below stays there; every egg she lays hatches inside. 42 units at first, 69 by 10:00, one
   of them for 245 seconds.
3. `03_redrock_before_7m00_ramp_closed.jpg`: 7:00, wider: the Clockwork army walks round outside and hits a Rootmaw at
   the top left; the Brood's army can't reach it.
4. `04_redrock_after_7m00_ramp_open.jpg`: the same map and plans after the fix, 7:00: nothing roots by the ramp's mouth
   (the turtle the alarm sent stands outside, top right); the way in and out is open both ways (here the Clockwork army
   comes up it and the Brood meets it). No unit was shut in in this game.
5. `05_cinder_before_7m00_growing.jpg`: Cinder Steps, Economy against the Crown Economy (the owner's eco v eco, seed
   11), before, 7:00. The main is in the map's corner (the black at the right is the map's edge), the ramp at the top.
   The Mother is GROWING to stage 3: her nest will grow to 12 x 5 tiles.
6. `06_cinder_before_8m01_shut_in.jpg`: the same game, 8:01: her grown nest, the Mound Turtles packed round her (supply
   turtles) and the cliff close the base: 95 units shut in, then 149 (two pockets), to the end of the game at 15:00,
   while the Crown's army grew to 5,190. Compare the owner's picture: the same shape, the same corner.
7. `07_cinder_s21_before_8m05_wide.jpg`: Cinder Steps again (seed 21), before, 8:05, wider: the ramp (top right of the
   Brood's corner), the turtle rooted beside the Mother right under it, and the crowd of its own units against the
   cliff at her left: 123 of them at once in this game. It happens in most Cinder Steps games before the fix.
8. `08_cinder_s21_before_9m50.jpg`: the same, 9:50: still shut in, eggs full (20/20).
9. `09_cinder_s21_after_9m50.jpg`: after the fix, same seed, 9:50: the turtles root away from the ramp's mouth and
   from the ground her stage 3 nest takes (one stands out on the left, at the foot of the cliff); the army comes and goes.
10. `10_cinder_after_10m00.jpg`: after the fix (seed 11), 10:00: the same base, open; this game was won at 13:33.
