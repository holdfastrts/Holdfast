# The Springer timing, in pictures (0.28.0; docs/clockwork_ai_rebuild.md)

Hard Clockwork computer on the Springer timing (blue) against the Hard Crown computer (red). Filmed with
tools/play_clockwork_springer.gd. Round A is the first draft; round B is the plan as it is now.

1. `01_build_greenhold_0414.jpg` (round B, Greenhold, 4:14): the build when the fourth Springer is out. The Clocktower in
   the middle; two Cog Presses with 5 Winders each; the Mainspring on its big gear with 6 Winders; the Escapement, two
   Light Works, the Clock Aviary and the Regulators; the Springers by the Works.
2. `02_swift_and_stage_kestrel_0505.jpg` (round B, Kestrel Rise, 5:05): the four Springers at the foot of the Crown's
   cliff, the Swift over them. The Swift gives the sight they need to leap up. Two Crown Farms just above.
3. `03_leap_into_the_farm_kestrel_0526.jpg` (round B, Kestrel Rise, 5:26 plus 8 s): the Springers landed in the nearer Farm
   and are shooting it; its farmers have run. The Musketeers come from the left. The two Winders wait below the cliff.
4. `04_farm_gone_leapt_out_kestrel_0559.jpg` (round B, Kestrel Rise, 5:59): the Farm is gone. The Springers leapt back
   down to the Winders, who wind and mend them.
5. `05_second_farm_down_kestrel_0835.jpg` (round B, Kestrel Rise, 8:35 plus 8 s): the third leap. A second Farm is rubble;
   Musketeers and a Horseman arrive; the Winders and the rest wait at the foot. The Crown's income stalled after these.
6. `06_round_a_farmers_flee_kestrel_0505.jpg` (round A, Kestrel Rise seed 12, 5:05): "Farmers 0/8". The farmers run from
   the Springers as fast as Springers walk, so few die; the damage is the work they lose.
7. `07_greenhold_musketeers_come_0508.jpg` (round A, Greenhold, 5:08 plus 8 s): why it is hard. Eight seconds after the
   leap, a line of Musketeers is already there (they reach farther than a Springer).
8. `08_greenhold_back_to_the_cranks_0523.jpg` (round A, Greenhold, 5:23): the leap out. Springers down at the cliff foot
   with the Winders; one lost up top.
9. `09_greenhold_farms_out_of_reach_0508.jpg` (round B, Greenhold, 5:08 plus 8 s): on Greenhold the Farms stand 7 to 9
   tiles in from the cliff, the leap is 5: the Springers land in the open and shoot at range.
10. `10_diversion_at_the_ramp_kestrel_0526.jpg` (round B, Kestrel Rise, 5:29): the diversion. Only one Clock Soldier was
    out in time; it stands below the Crown's ramp. It drew nothing away (open question).
11. `11_first_leap_kestrel.gif` (round B, Kestrel Rise, from 5:26): the first leap in, filmed: the crouch, the leap, the
    Springers in the Farm.
