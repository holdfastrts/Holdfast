# Pictures: the Clock Soldier rush and the Turret turtle (cw-builds-a, docs/clockwork_builds_a.md)

All from coached, filmed games: the Clockwork computer (blue, Hard) against the Crown or the Brood computer (red, Hard),
seed 11. The camera sees everything; the computers only see what their side sees.

1. `01_rush_out.jpg` (Greenhold, Crown, 2:56): the rush on its way: six Clock Soldiers with the two Winders that wind and
   mend them walking behind.
2. `02_roundA_on_the_ramp.jpg` (Greenhold, Crown, about 3:40, before the last fix): what the films showed first. The
   soldiers climbed the Crown's ramp and pressed against its gate and Cottage while three Musketeers shot them from inside.
   Four died for nothing. Now the party decides at the foot, as soon as it sees the gate.
3. `03_held_wall_watch_at_foot.jpg` (Greenhold, Crown, 4:10): the rush called FAILED at a held wall; the party watches the
   foot of the ramp. The Crown's Musketeers came out through the gate to chase it (right): the fight happens in the open.
4. `04_kestrel_rush_success.jpg` (Kestrel Rise, Crown, 6:00, round A): the one rush that got in. The Crown had walled the
   front but the soldiers found the workers: by 6:00 the Crown had one Labourer left and its farms stood empty.
5. `05_rush_v_brood_ramp.jpg` (Greenhold, Brood, 3:43): against the Brood the rush meets a rooted defender and Raptors on
   the ramp. The Brood rush loses; see "What is weak" in the doc.
6. `06_roundA_turret_too_far.jpg` (Greenhold, Crown, 2:10, round A): the first turtle. The Light Works stood right above the
   ramp, its big gear ring took the room, and the only turret spot the gears reached was about 300 px from the ramp's top,
   out of its 200 px reach.
7. `07_turtle_kestrel_turrets.jpg` (Kestrel Rise, Crown, 2:46): after the fix. The Regulator goes toward the ramp first
   (the gear field's stepping stone), the Works behind the Clocktower; the turrets stand at the ramp's top, and the second
   Clocktower is going up below the ramp (bottom).
8. `08_turtle_frostmere_turrets.jpg` (Frostmere, Crown, 2:55): the same build on another map: a turret 50 px from the
   choke, the second one on the other side (their 4-tile circles may not overlap), the new Clocktower below.
9. `09_turtle_v_brood_natural.jpg` (Greenhold, Brood, 5:36): Raptors come at the natural; its turret (the domed clock
   tower) and the first soldiers meet them.
10. `10_turtle_v_brood_held.jpg` (Greenhold, Brood, 8:07): a big Brood attack at the natural, held: the turret is burning,
    but dead Raptors lie all round and the Clockwork army stands.
11. `11_turtle_wave_kestrel.jpg` (Kestrel Rise, Crown, 8:56): the turtle's first wave, later and bigger, burns down the
    Crown's expansion.
12. `12_turtle_v_brood_roundA.jpg` (Greenhold, Brood, 7:27, round A): the turret by the second Clocktower in a fight with
    Raptors at the foot of the ramp, the army coming down to help.
13. `13_rush_at_the_wall.gif` (Greenhold, Crown, from 3:34): the rush reaching the Crown's walled ramp: the soldiers hit the
    gate under the Musketeers' fire, then the party backs off.
