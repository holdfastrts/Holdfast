# Pictures: the Brood computer retuned (docs/brood_ai_tune.md)

All from the coached, filmed games of this job (Hard Brood computer against a Hard Crown or Clockwork computer).
The game number (a = round A, b = round B, c = round C) and the clock are in each line.

- **01_natural_turtle_0033.jpg** (b03, Dune Sea, against the Crown): the first Mound Turtle rooted on the natural at
  0:33, its swamp spreading round it. Every economic plan does this first; it is still the right opening.
- **02_mother_stage2_sires_0700.jpg** (b02, Dune Sea, against the Clockwork): the Mother at 7:00, grown, with the
  Tyrant Sire on his nest (the fire) below her and her Rootmaws and Mound Turtles round her swamp. Since this job her
  stages, the Venom Sire and the next turtles come as soon as her clutch runs dry (eggs, not food, hold the Brood back
  on today's economy).
- **03_roundA_full_clutch_sire_away_1000.jpg** (a05, round A, Greenhold, against the Crown): what round A found. Her
  clutch sits FULL (Eggs 19/20 over her) with Musketeers at her nest, because the Tyrant Sire is away chasing riders
  who keep out of his reach: no Raptor egg can be laid while he is off his nest. 4,000 food piled up. Fixed for round
  B: a Sire in the defence goes back to his nest once only riders are left, 16 tiles from her, or after 30 seconds.
- **04_clock_soldiers_at_the_nest_0514.jpg** (a06, Highmoor, against the Clockwork): 15 Clock Soldiers at the Mother's
  nest at 5:14. The Tyrant Sire is up fighting them (left); a struck Rootmaw is UPROOTING to back off (right). This is
  the Clockwork's early army, the Brood's hardest moment in every game against it.
- **05_mother_up_for_a_real_attack_0627.jpg** (a06): the Mother got up and fights them with the Tyrant Sire. This is a
  correct uproot (a real attack bigger than the army at home); in the 36 filmed games she never got up for a scout or a
  small harass.
- **06_roundC_alarm_turtle_walks_into_the_fight_0638.jpg** (c10, round C, Greenhold, against the Clockwork): a turtle
  misused. The alarm sent a Mound Turtle from home "to root in front of the natural" at 6:33 while 12 Clock Soldiers were
  already at the Mother: it walks into them uprooted (bottom right, among the soldiers), its supply gone. Fixed after
  round C: no alarm turtle once the attack is at home, and one that meets enemies on its way roots where it stands.
- **07_raptors_up_the_clockwork_ramp_0732.jpg** (b02): the Brood's second wave pouring up the Clockwork's ramp at
  7:32 into its gear base, Clockwork wrecks on the ramp. The game was won at 11:58.
- **08_raptors_at_a_clocktower_0817.jpg** (a04, Twin Fords, against the Clockwork): Raptors tearing at a Clocktower at
  8:17 (won at 10:23).
- **09_raptors_burn_a_crown_hall_0810.jpg** (a01, Highmoor, against the Crown): a Raptor wave at a burning Crown Town
  Hall at 8:10. This game was a DRAW in round A although the Crown had no army from 11:00: one hidden Cottage kept it
  alive while the 135 Raptors walked between two empty points. In round B the same game was WON at 14:37 (the wave
  splits into packs that look for what is left).
- **10_raptors_hunt_musketeers_0646.jpg** (b05, Greenhold, against the Crown): the first wave catching Musketeers out
  by a Crown building at 6:46.
- **11_crown_riders_raid_an_expansion_1223.jpg** (b01): Crown riders and Musketeers raiding a Brood expansion at 12:23;
  the grubs have left their Rootmaw (GRUBS 0/4) and the Raptors answer.
- **12_riders_on_the_mothers_ramp_0403.jpg** (b11, Twin Fords, against the Crown): two Horsemen on the ramp to the
  Mother's plateau at 4:03, and the alarm's Mound Turtle UPROOTING to root in front of the natural (before the fix of
  picture 06: since then a turtle only goes before the attack reaches home).
- **13_roundC_rush_turned_balanced_wave_0519.jpg** (c08, round C, Twin Fords, against the Clockwork): the Rush plan's
  second wave at 5:19, after its rush came home and it turned Balanced. In round B this game was lost at 13:01 on
  3,862 food earned; in round C it was a draw that traded 10,695 for 5,205 and out-earned the Clockwork.
- **14_first_wave_vs_clockwork.gif** (b02, 15 frames): the first wave leaving its staging point at the ramp foot past
  the natural (Raptors in a pack, blue), against the Clockwork.
