# The Owl raids (cw-builds-c): pictures

Clockwork computer (blue) on the Owl raids, Hard, against the Hard Crown (red) or the Hard Brood. Filmed with
tools/play_clockwork_owls.gd. Round A: Greenhold seed 11, before the fixes. Round B: Kestrel Rise seed 11, after them.

- 01_first_owl.jpg: Greenhold, 5:07. The first Ticking Owl flies, by the two Clock Aviaries (the tall clock towers at the
  middle and right of the base).
- 02_map_greenhold.jpg: Greenhold, 5:33, the whole map. The Clockwork main in the top left corner, the Crown's in the bottom
  right. An Owl runs down in about 80 seconds off its gear field: from home it cannot get there and back. Hence the crank
  post.
- 03_roundA_cranks_lost.jpg: round A, 6:42. The Owls reach the Crown's gate (top left), low on wind; the two cranks that
  walked out alone lie dead in the field below (the two wrecks).
- 04_roundA_rider_at_post.jpg: round A, the first crank post in the middle of the map. The Crown's rider scout rides past:
  he killed the cranks walking there alone. Now the flock flies over them.
- 05_roundA_post_by_crown_hall.jpg: round A, 8:18. The post the computer picked was where the Crown builds its next Town
  Hall, with its Musketeers round it. Now the post keeps away from the natural expansions and from all it has seen.
- 06_roundA_brood_overrun.jpg: round A against the Brood, 7:11. Raptors among the Clockwork buildings, the Owls above them.
  Raptors cannot hit flyers: the Owls killed 140 of them in that game, but the base fell while the flock was out. Now a
  real attack on the base calls the flock back from anywhere.
- 07_roundA_post_by_brood.jpg: round A against the Brood, a post picked next to a Brood expansion (the rooted creature and
  its swamp). Now the Swift looks at the post first.
- 08_roundB_crown_defend.jpg: round B, Kestrel Rise, 6:34. The Owls over the Clockwork's base by its Clocktower, seeing
  off a Crown scout (in red, top right). Most of what the Owls do against the Crown is guarding the base against riders.
- 09_roundB_crown_strike.jpg: round B, 8:33. An Owl (top left) at the Crown's new Town Hall: Musketeers all round it. The
  flock turned back four seconds later (anti-air 325 against 340).
- 10_roundB_brood_out.jpg: round B against the Brood, 7:02. Three Owls fly over the Brood's Rootmaws toward its grubs.
- 11_roundB_brood_strike.jpg: 7:11, the strike: the Owls at a Rootmaw by the Brood's ramp.
- 12_roundB_brood_fight.jpg: 7:36, the Owls by the Brood's natural; its Raptors cannot touch them.
- 13_roundB_base_6min.jpg: the Clockwork base at 6:00 on Kestrel Rise: the Mainspring with its six Winders (right), the
  red-roofed Clock Aviaries, the Light Works (the blue frames), a Swift over the Clocktower.
- 14_first_strike_brood.gif: the first strike against the Brood (Kestrel Rise, 7:11), about 6 seconds.
