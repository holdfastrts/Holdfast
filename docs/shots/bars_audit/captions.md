- brood_closeups.jpg: the Brood's rooting creatures: the Rootmaw (its food and ground bars were on its back), the Mound

- mother_stages.jpg and mother_stage_closeups.jpg: the Mother rooted at stages 1, 2 and 3, grown where she stands and

  rooted loop's breathing), centred over her, and stay put while she lays.
