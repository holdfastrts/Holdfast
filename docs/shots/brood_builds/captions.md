# The Brood's written builds (brood-builds): pictures

From the coached, filmed games of docs/brood_builds.md (tools/play_brood_builds.gd, 1280x720 shrunk to 1200 px). The
Brood is side 0 (its creatures carry the player colour blue); the opponent a Hard computer. Game names (a01, d04...)
are the rows of the tables in docs/brood_builds.md.

## Spitter timing

- `01_timing_meets_clockwork_highmoor.jpg` (d02, Highmoor 5, Clockwork, 7:50): the timing (13 Spitters, 26 Raptors and
  Challengers) left at 7:38 and met the Clockwork's army 18 tiles out, at a Brood expansion it was attacking (right);
  the Spitters spit from the left while the Raptors bite. The push killed 8,155; the Brood won at 11:41 (Balanced drew
  this game).
- `02_timing_spits_crown_field.jpg` (d01, Greenhold 11, Crown, 7:39): the timing reaches the Crown's side outside its
  wall: the Spitters (centre) spit at a Musketeer squad in the field by the Crown's expansion; the Crown's ramp gate is
  at the bottom right. It took what was outside the wall and came home at 8:31 ("what is left is behind the wall").
- `03_timing_razes_crown_mill.jpg` (b01, Greenhold 11, Crown, 8:30): the round B timing broke the Crown's expansion
  outside the wall (its Mill fading, top left) while the Musketeers stand inside, below the ramp.
- `04_bug_timing_at_the_gate.jpg` (b01, 9:55, the bug round B showed): the same timing then piled at the Crown's ramp
  gate (top) under the musket line and lost half its Raptors there. Fixed: with no Brutes it comes home once what is
  left is behind a wall.
- `05_timing_meets_clockwork_ford.jpg` (d04, Twin Fords 9, Clockwork, 8:39): the timing (17 Spitters, 25 Raptors) meets
  the Clockwork's Clock Soldiers and Springers at the ford. The push killed 8,600; the Brood won at 12:21 (every Brood
  plan had lost or drawn this game before, Balanced too).
- `06_timing_contact_clockwork_greenhold.jpg` (d06, Greenhold 3, Clockwork, 8:35): contact at the foot of a ramp; the
  Raptors go up the ramp at a Clockwork building (right), the Spitters spit from below. The push killed 9,915.
- `12_first_draft_timing_at_crown_ramp.jpg` (a03, Dune Sea 7, Crown, 8:55, round A): the first draft's timing broke the
  building outside the Crown's ramp (centre) and fought the Musketeers at the gate.
- `spitter_timing_march.gif` (d02): the march at the Spitters' pace, the Raptors just ahead of them.

## Brute siege

- `07_brutes_batter_crown_gate.jpg` (the trial game, Greenhold 3, Crown, 8:47): the Brutes (four, with Battering Horns)
  batter the Crown's ramp gate; the Spitters behind them spit at the Musketeers inside; the Raptors wait below.
- `08_breach_swarm_pours_in.jpg` (the same game, 8:59): BREACH: the gate is down and the swarm pours into the Crown's
  main past the Barracks. The Crown was out at 10:07 (6,800 killed for 1,540).
- `09_brutes_break_turret_dune_sea.jpg` (b08, Dune Sea 7, Clockwork, 10:41): the Brutes break a Ratchet Turret (fading,
  top) on the edge of the Clockwork's gear field; the army goes in at 10:48. Won at 12:38.
- `10_brutes_at_clockwork_main.jpg` (a06, Greenhold 3, Clockwork, 12:25): 138 supply with 6 Brutes at the Clockwork's
  main: the Brutes at the turret by the Clocktower, the swarm round them. The push killed 9,930 for 2,500 lost.
- `11_brutes_inside_crown_main.jpg` (b07, Twin Fords 9, Crown, 9:29): the Brutes inside the Crown's main among the
  ruins of its buildings; the ramp gate (top) fell to them. Won at 10:35.
- `brutes_break_the_gate.gif` (the trial game, 8:47): the Brutes at the gate until it falls.
