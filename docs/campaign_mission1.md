# Mission 1: The Leaf and the Fire (Virginia, 2767)

APPROVED by the owner on 2026-11-04 20:50 ("Yes do it"). The campaign's first mission, told in flashback by the King.
Tone (owner): Catholic and monarchist; the Crown and the Church are objectively the good guys; the King narrates truthfully.

## People

- KING CONSTANTINE IV, in Rome, the narrator (the present, an old man with a pipe).
- LORD EDMUND FAIRLEIGH, Governor of Virginia: English, stern, a veteran, never raises his voice.
- TOMASZ WIERZBICKI, a young Polish seminarian waiting for his ship to Rome; later the missionary priest. The mission's
  witness and second narrator.
- CAPTAIN MATTEO RUGGIERI, Italian, commands the guns.
- ETIENNE VARENNE, French orator, leader of the FREE ASSEMBLY (rebels against Church and Crown: "freedom" and
  secularism; colours GREEN AND BLACK). A minor, non-playable faction with its own units and art.

## Opening (over the oil-painting stills, art/campaign/stills/p01_01..05)

1. King lighting his pipe (p01_01). KING: "You ask how the world came to burn. Sit. Let an old man light his pipe
   first. The leaf is from Virginia. It is fitting. That is where it began."
2. The world map (p01_02). KING: "In those days the sun did not set on the Crown. From Rome to the farthest islands
   there was one faith, one law, one King. We were not perfect. But we were at peace, and we thought peace was the
   natural state of things."
3. The plantation (p01_03). KING: "Virginia gave us its golden leaf, and gave its sons to the seminaries. That summer
   a young Pole, Tomasz Wierzbicki, waited there for his ship to Rome." TOMASZ: "I remember the smell of the drying
   barns, and how quiet the evenings were. I was reading Augustine under the oak. I thought I knew what evil was."
4. The rebels (p01_04). KING: "Then came the men who called themselves the Free Assembly. Free of God, free of their
   King, free of every bond that had kept them fed and safe. They began, as such men always do, with fire."
5. The Governor (p01_05). FAIRLEIGH: "Captain Ruggieri, run out the guns. No man burns the King's leaf while I stand on
   these steps." TOMASZ: "He did not raise his voice. He did not need to."

## Part 1: Hold until dawn (night)

- The player defends the manor, the chapel and the drying barns (the Farms are tobacco fields/drying barns in this
  mission). Start: a Barracks, a few Musketeers, two Field Cannons; train more as waves come out of the tobacco fields.
- Win the part: the manor and at least three barns stand at dawn (about 8 minutes).
- During the waves: VARENNE shouts slogans ("No king but the people!"), RUGGIERI answers with the guns, TOMASZ notices
  the dead on both sides. The mob must read as armed and destructive (it burns fields and kills loyal people), so the
  Crown's severity is just.

## Part 2: The road to the fort (dawn)

- The rebels fall back to the province fort they took. March and clear two rebel camps on the road.
- CHOICE (seeds a later mission): a column of loyal families is trapped at a burning farm off the road. Save them (you
  arrive later; the rebels are dug in better) or leave them (Tomasz remembers it).

## Part 3: The gate

- The rebels mass at the fort gate. Bring the cannons within range, load CANISTER. FAIRLEIGH: "Canister. On my word."
  When the mob charges, fire.

## Ending (over p01_06, the canister volley)

- TOMASZ: "I prayed for them that morning, and I pray for them still. But I never again believed that disorder was a
  kind of mercy."
- KING (the pipe gone out): "Fairleigh ended the rising in a single morning. We thought it was over. It was the first
  spark."
