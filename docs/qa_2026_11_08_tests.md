# QA study, 8 November 2026: the automated test suite (job qa-tests)

Part of the owner's "overall study and complete systematic test of the game" (2026-11-07). This job owns the automated
tests in tools/test_*.gd; four other jobs look at content, computer matches, the UI and performance. Branch `qa-tests`
from main 0.33.7; the result is 0.33.8.

The per-test table is docs/qa_2026_11_08/tests.csv (every test: its verdict on 0.33.7 as it was, its verdict now, every
run in between, its run time, script errors and its first failing lines).

## How it was run

Godot 4.7 headless, `--fixed-fps 30`, every test in its own process, two at a time, each with its own user folder
(XDG_DATA_HOME), so no test reads another's save file (the 10-27 QA saw test_hotkeys and test_horses fail only beside
others: they share user://). A limit of 30 minutes a test, and a watchdog that stops a test whose output has been silent
for 10 minutes (a test that stopped on an error but never quit). test_online has its own script (tools/test_online.sh:
a relay, a host and a guest process) and was run separately for a Crown, a Brood and a Clockwork guest.

243 tests (tools/test_*.gd without test_online), plus test_online three times, plus the boot check and three full
computer games watched for runtime errors.

## Real bugs found

Fixed (safe and local):

1. **Card lines too long** (test_card_text): five card lines were over the 80-character limit and ran off the card: the
   Great Winder in all three languages (86, 112, 119 characters), the Marching Band in Portuguese (84) and the
   Mainspring in Portuguese (87). Shortened (scripts/defs.gd; tools/i18n/39_card_text.txt, 42_tension_net.txt,
   56_marching_band_unit.txt; the language files rebuilt). Player-visible: in the patch notes.
2. **A narrator line that never showed** (test_spectator): the AI story's phrase "<player>'s scout was stopped before it
   saw anything" (scripts/ai_story_lines.gd, rule 61) captured a word its sentence never used, so the sentence was
   formatted with one argument too many: an error, and the line was lost from the spectator's story. The capture is now
   non-capturing. Player-visible (spectators): in the patch notes.

Not fixed (described for the owner; most are the computer's play, which is other jobs' ground and the owner's design):

3. **The Clockwork computer stalls for good on spent brass** (test_ai_expand, both runs). Cinder Steps, a Hard Balanced
   Clockwork with its other halls razed at 4:00, its bank cut to 150 and its brass to 8%: for about 30 s its build order
   (AIClockwork.bo_on: AIClockwork._build_up runs _bo_tick and never AI._hall_first) keeps training Clock Soldiers and
   Winders, the brass runs out at about 1:00 with 440 cogs in the bank, and then AI._hall_first (scripts/ai.gd ~2569)
   wants the hall plus its first press, 400 + 140 = 540, on dry land. With no income left the bank never grows: 21 of 24
   Winders idle for the last three minutes, no Clocktower laid. (The Crown passes the same test: it lays a hall at 34 s.)
   Two ways out: let the build order hand over to _hall_first once the land is dry (save the 540 before more units), or,
   with nothing coming in, lay the hall alone when the bank holds its price (the press then waits, but the hall alone
   earns nothing either, so the first is the real fix). Design principles 2 (depletion forces expansion; a barren
   computer is no opponent). Left for the owner / an AI job because it changes the build orders' rules.
4. **The Crown's walled natural is late** (test_ai_expand_wall, three runs: 10-28% of the ways in shut 180 s after the
   hall stood, the expansion's core still reachable 240 s after; the gate missing on two maps in the first run). The
   palisade is planned at once ("plans palisade for an empty wall slot ... laid later") and laid much later. CLAUDE.md
   already lists "the late natural wall" as open (AI FOLLOW-UPS). Before this job the test's "sealed" check could not
   fail: its helper crashed on the new shape of `_foe_reach` and returned 0 (fixed in the test).
5. **The Clockwork computer no longer harasses** (test_ai_harass [clock], three runs). Since clockwork-army (ONE ARMY,
   AIClockwork._army_one) the shared harass (scripts/ai_harass.gd) is never ticked for the Clockwork; a lone Clock
   Soldier out by the Brood is attack-moved home to its gathering point and stands at a ROOTED Mound Turtle for the
   whole 40 s: the owner's complaint of 2026-10-31 that the harass was made for. The Crown's Horseman and raid cases
   pass. A design question: should ONE ARMY hand lone soldiers far from home to the harass again, or walk them home with
   a plain move (no attack-move into rooted creatures)?
6. **The Great Winder of the computer never sweeps** (test_great_winder, three runs): sent with the army it stands 230-330
   px from it ("just behind": wants under 140-170), and in 15 s by the army it sweeps no fallen soldier (swept 0, +0 cogs).
   Since clockwork-army the Great Winder belongs to the push and the home rest spots (BACK_GW 190 px), which keep giving
   it MOVE orders, and the sweep is an idle job. The owner's rule "the Clockwork regains cogs from bodies" does not

## Claude's choices for the owner

- A test is "stale" only where CLAUDE.md or a doc names the change as deliberate; everything else that fails is reported
  as the game's behaviour, even when it is probably a test that needs new numbers (items 3-16 above).
- test_ai_harass, test_ai_rush_followup, test_great_winder, test_crown_dragoon_raid and test_cw_air_ai were left failing
  rather than loosened: each fails on a rule the owner set (harass at the worker line, no lone riders, the Clockwork's
  cogs from bodies, raids that ride, a quick air answer), and changing the test would hide the question.
- The Clockwork study tests now run their check when called with no arguments (any argument keeps the old study mode).
- The shelved Line tests were kept, not deleted.
- The planted-base AI tests end the computer's build order first: the shared rules they test are what the computer runs
  after its opening, which is what the test means; the plans' own builds are tested by test_clockwork_builds_a/b.
- Two engine runs at a time with their own user folders; the online test with its script, as the 10-27 QA did.
