# QA of the interface, 8 November 2026 (job qa-ui)

One of the five jobs of the owner's "overall study and complete systematic test of the game" (2026-11-07). This one owns
everything the player clicks outside the battle rules: the menus, Settings, the skirmish setup, the HUD, the end
screen, the tutorial, the campaign, the map editor, saving and loading, replays, online play and the web version.
It builds on docs/qa_2026_10_27.md, docs/qa_web.md and docs/qa_soak.md. Tested on 0.33.7 (main), fixes in **0.33.8**
(branch `qa-ui`). Pictures: `docs/shots/qa_2026_11_08/` ("before_..." shows a problem as found, "after_..." the fix).

## How it was tested

- **Every screen, four sizes, three languages.** A new tool, `tools/qa_ui_audit.gd`, opens every page in turn (the
  device page, the main menu, the five Settings tabs scrolled to the end, PATCH NOTES, the music player, the interface
  layout page, the animation viewer, the seven HOW TO PLAY sections, the skirmish setup as 1v1, 2v2, FFA, 1v3 and 4v4
  (and with `maps`: every one of the 27 maps), ONLINE, REPLAYS, the EDITOR page, MATCH LOGS, the CAMPAIGN hub, the
  TUTORIAL list and the MAP EDITOR), then plays a match as each faction (the HUD with the hall, one worker and all the
  workers selected, the in-game MENU, paused), the end screen, the main menu with CONTINUE, and a 4-player computer
  match to watch (AI STORY, PRODUCTION, ARMY & STRUCTURES, VIEW). For each screen it saves a picture and checks every
  Label and Button on it: text cut by its box, text off the screen, two texts on top of each other, words squeezed
  letter by letter, and (pt / es) English that has no translation. It ran at 1920x1080 and 1280x720 as a computer and
  2400x1080 and 1600x720 as a phone, in English, Portuguese and Spanish: 12 runs, about 60 pictures each, plus a run
  with every map. The HUD itself is drawn by hand (not with Labels), so for it the pictures were looked at.
- **Every button.** `tools/qa_ui_press_all.gd` presses every button of every menu page one at a time (the page shown
  fresh before each press), as a computer (407 presses on 21 pages) and as a phone (429), and reports a SCRIPT ERROR or
  a button wired to nothing. A press that starts a match or a lesson is followed by a return to the menu.
- **Every setting saves.** `tools/test_qa_settings_persist.gd` uses every Settings row the way a player does (presses
  its button, moves its slider, types the relay address), on the computer and the phone, and checks it changes, is in
  settings.cfg (read back from the file), and that pressing a switch twice puts it back; the volume bars are read back
  from the music player's own section; the language flags; RESET (armed, then pressed).
- **Saves come back identical.** `tools/test_qa_save_roundtrip.gd` plays 3:20 as each faction (and a 3-player match),
  captures the whole state, CONTINUEs it as the main menu does and captures it again at once: every unit, building,
  bank, queue and research compared field by field. Then the match plays on.
- **The existing tests of these areas**, all run: menu grid, Settings tabs, Settings in a match, i18n, save and
  continue, save job, the tutorial (every lesson played), the campaign engine, campaign mission 1 (both ways of the
  families choice, to the end), replays FULL, LIGHT and MIXED (play back, seek, speed, pause, a cut-short file, an old
  version), replay names, the map editor (make, CHECK, save, load, PLAY, a match on it) and its buttons, hotkeys and
  control groups, watching computers, the spectator panels, online in loopback (host + guest through the local relay,
  versus and co-op), HUD frames, the selection panel, the card text, card keys, group keys, drag release, the update
  check, the game name, camera margin, phone crew, cursors, unit stats, and the command-card icon audit for each faction.
- **The web build**, made the way CI makes it (`tools/web/build_web.sh`), served locally and played in headless
  Chromium with software WebGL by a new script, `tools/web/qa_smoke.cjs`: the device page, the main menu, a skirmish
  started, played a minute, the in-game MENU, then a reload, at a computer's size (1280x720, mouse) and a phone's
  (915x412, touch).

## Not fixed: for the owner or a later job

1. **Godot aborts when a headless test quits** ("Parameter RenderingServer::get_singleton() is null" in
   ~CompressedTexture2D, then Aborted, exit code 134): every test prints its result first, so the results are right,
   but a script reading the exit code sees 134. It is the engine freeing textures after its renderer; not seen in the
   game itself.
2. **Small menu fonts at 1280x720 in pt / es** (above): the grid's button size is the owner's (8.2% of the width, 100
   pixels at least, chosen to keep the grid in the painting's sky). 120 pixels would let CONFIGURAÇÕES and ESCARAMUÇA
   read at about the size of the other buttons and still stay left of the title's end. Left for the owner.
3. **Classic map previews** in the skirmish setup are a plain sketch while the named maps have a picture: a picture
   of a generated map would need it generated (it is made at match start from its seed).
4. **A grub's paid MORPH order is not saved**: after CONTINUE the grub stands and the food is paid back. Harmless, but
   not "identical"; saving the order would need the morph's target in the save.
5. **The spectator test's army count** failed once in three runs (a count taken in the frame a unit dies or hatches)
   and passed twice; a test timing matter, not seen on screen.
6. **Music titles, map names and match logs** stay English in pt / es (an earlier decision, said in the patch notes).
