# The band's music: the owner's reference pieces (2026-11-14)

The owner sent six recordings as the model for the Marching Band's music ("you will need to get inspiration from real
music"; "you can use the sounds themselves as notes, since it's sounds of real instruments"). They are NOT in the
repository (they are other people's recordings); this is what was measured from them (librosa, 60 s from 0:10 each):

| Piece | Instruments | Tempo (beats a minute) | Key (estimated) |
|---|---|---|---|
| American Civil War music, Fifes and Drums | fifes, snare and bass drums | ~117 | A-flat (minor read) |
| Highland Laddie / Black Bear | Highland pipes and drums | ~99 | B-flat |
| Scotland the Brave | Highland pipes and drums | ~99 | B-flat major |
| The British Grenadiers | fife and drum | ~108 | F / F-sharp area |
| March of the Preobrazhensky Regiment | fifes and drums | ~103 | D minor |
| Loch Lomond (Pipes and Drums of Leanisch) | Highland pipes and drums | ~136 (likely 68 counted twice) | E-flat |

What follows from it:
- Highland pipes are tuned sharp: their "A" sounds near B-flat (about 470-480 Hz), which is why every pipe piece reads as
  B-flat. A band with pipes must be in the pipes' key: the fife and the drums fit themselves to the pipes, not the other
  way round. One key for all three parts ends the cacophony (today's three 4 s loops share no key or tempo).
- The marching pace is ~100-118 beats a minute (quick march); pipe bands sit near 100, fife-and-drum corps near 110-120.
- The tunes themselves (The British Grenadiers, Scotland the Brave, Highland Laddie, Loch Lomond, the Preobrazhensky
  March, the Civil War camp tunes) are old and public domain as melodies; an arrangement played by us with licensed
  instrument samples is ours to use. Cutting single notes out of these particular recordings would copy the performers'
  recordings: only with a recording whose licence allows it (to be checked per file with the owner).
- Earlier references from the owner (already used for the fife and drums): fife_music, La Grenadiere, The Girl I Left
  Behind Me, Yankee Doodle (fife), the nesrality marching drum solos 1-5 (Pixabay), snare and bass drum effects.
