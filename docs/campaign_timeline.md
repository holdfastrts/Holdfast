# The canon timeline (for the writers)

PROPOSAL by Claude, 2026-11-11, built on docs/campaign_structure.md and the owner's decisions in CLAUDE.md. Names,
dates and places marked (proposed) are Claude's; the owner confirms or changes them. Plot only: all dialogue is the
owner's to write.

The canon is the Crown's account (the King narrates truthfully). Where a choice splits the story, the canon branch is
named; the other branch is a real, playable version, but the writers check against this line.

## Dates (proposed)

The Prologue is 2767 (owner). The King was young and newly crowned then; the present is about 2795.

| year | act | event |
|---|---|---|
| 2767 | Prologue | Virginia: the Free Assembly burns the tobacco country; Fairleigh ends the rising with canister at the fort gate. Varenne escapes in the smoke. |
| 2767-2770 | Act 1 | The Assembly spreads (Paris above all). The Guild of the Escapement builds labouring automata for the Crown. Valadares climbs a lost plateau in the Amazon and finds the creatures; Lindqvist's academy takes specimens. Varenne is taken at the barricades of Paris: spared or executed. |
| 2770 | 1st crossing | THE COUNCIL OF ROME judges the machines and the creatures. Ferrer and Valadares testify. The verdict: no machine may govern itself; the specimens are to be destroyed. Hollweg and Lindqvist both defy it. |
| 2771-2780 | Act 2 | THE BREAKING. The guild leaves the Crown and founds its own city of gears (proposed: in the south of Brazil). Lindqvist breeds the creatures in secret; they get loose and the Brood is born around a Queen. |
| about 2780 | 2nd crossing | BRAZIL BETWEEN TWO FIRES: a Brood swarm on the Amazon border, the Clockwork city on the southern border. The Crown chooses which to fight first (a diamond branch). |
| 2781-2790 | Act 3 | THE FALL OF THE COLONIES. Both dangers grow; colonies fall one by one; rebels join one side or the other. |
| about 2790 | 3rd crossing | THE GLOBAL WAR BEGINS: the Clockwork and the Brood turn on the Crown at once, and on each other. |
| about 2795 | Finale | The present. The King has told it all; the heir takes command. One of the two endings. |

## The two endings

- ROME HOLDS: the empire, smaller, endures; the heir is crowned.
- ROME FALLS, THE FAITH CARRIES ON: the court in exile, the Church surviving.
Which one depends on the ledger; the minor variations (who is alive, which places stand, who stands with you) come from
it too.
