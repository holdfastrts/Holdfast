REAL INSTRUMENT SAMPLES FOR THE MARCHING BAND'S MUSIC (fetched 2026-11-14 by the job band-v2; not used by the game yet:
the music job to come builds the band's loops from them; docs/band_v2.md has the brief). Source material only: this
folder has a .gdignore, so Godot never imports it and nothing here goes into a build.

bagpipe_freepats/   the FreePats "Bagpipe" set, version 2026-08-06 (SFZ + FLAC, 29 files as published)
  licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication), see bagpipe_freepats/LICENSE.txt
  author:  Gilles Sadowski (recorded August 2020, on a bagpipe in G designed by Remy Dubois and built by Olle Geris)
  link:    https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Ethnic/bagpipe.html
           https://github.com/freepats/bagpipe/releases/download/2026-08-06/Bagpipe-SFZ+FLAC-20260806.7z
  what:    chanter notes F4..G5 (two takes each, *_31 / *_32), and the two drones (drone_G2, drone_G3); the .sfz maps them.
  note:    this is a bagpipe in G (a continental pipe), NOT a Highland pipe (Highland pipes sound near B-flat): see the
           brief in docs/band_v2.md for what that means for the key.

vcsl/               from the Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL), converted WAV -> FLAC (lossless), folders kept
  licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication), see vcsl/LICENSE.txt
  author:  Versilian Studios (Sam Gossner) and contributors
  link:    https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL
  what:    Membranophones/Struck Membranophones/Snare Drum, Rope Tension (Hi, Low): a rope-tension snare = the field drum
             of the period, the best match for the Drummer
           Membranophones/Struck Membranophones/Legacy Snares/drum3_marching: a marching snare
           Membranophones/Struck Membranophones/Bass Drum 1: a bass drum (the beat under the snare in a pipe band)
           Aerophones/Edge-blown Aerophones/Baroque Soprano Recorder (Sustain, Staccato): the nearest CC0 instrument to a
             fife found on GitHub (no CC0 fife or piccolo set was found; the owner's earlier real fife notes are in
             art/audio_src/fife/ and tools/audio/fife_sampler.py)

USED (job band-music, 0.36.6; tools/audio/make_band_music.py, docs/band_music.md): the FreePats chanter notes and both
drones (bagpipe_freepats/ is the same set as freepats_bagpipe/: the owner's WAVs in freepats_bagpipe/ were used), the
MuldjordKit snare, rim, kick, floor tom and crash (muldjord_drums/, CC BY 4.0: credit kept in docs/audio_credits.md), and
the VCSL Baroque Soprano Recorder's Sustain notes as the fife. The VCSL snares and bass drum are not used (the job
brief picked the MuldjordKit, with its 56 snare layers). Not used: the owner's fife notes in art/audio_src/fife/notes/
(cut from a band recording: the other fifes and the drums are in every note, see docs/band_music.md).
